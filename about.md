# About Us: Scientific Research and Communication Platform

**Science Portal** is an independent digital scientific journal platform established for the exploration, analysis, and dissemination of physical sciences. Our primary mission is to support the scientific creativity of young researchers, students, and professionals, connecting them within a unified academic network.

### Our Mission and Strategy
Our platform's core mission is to foster a free academic environment while upholding the principles of scientific integrity. We believe that fundamental science should not be confined to laboratories and textbooks but should be reflected in the individual scientific creativity of every mind.

### Editorial Policy and Scientific Review
Unlike open blog sites, **Science Portal** operates as an **e-journal**. This format ensures the quality and credibility of every published material:

* **Scientific Expertise:** Every submitted article is analyzed by our editorial team for scientific fundamentality, logical consistency, and accuracy.
* **Classification:** Submissions are categorized based on their scientific value and specific subject matter.
* **Author Identification:** Every piece of writing on our platform is officially published with the **author's name and scientific status** (student, researcher, etc.) clearly credited. This serves as a vital foundation for our authors' future academic portfolios.

### Call for Authors
We welcome submissions from anyone with a passion for physics—be they students, teachers, or amateur researchers. If you have an interesting scientific finding, a theoretical proposition, or experimental notes, we invite you to send them to us. Should your writing meet our scientific criteria, it will be presented to our journal's permanent readership.
